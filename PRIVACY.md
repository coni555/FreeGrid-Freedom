# FreeGrid 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-31

## 中文

FreeGrid 是一款账本存在本机的记账应用。我们对隐私的承诺很简单:

- **不收集任何数据。** FreeGrid 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人信息或财务数据。
- **账本不会传给我们。** App 只有两处联网:FreeGrid Pro 的购买与恢复购买,由 Apple 的 App Store 完成;iCloud 自动备份(默认打开,可以在「备份与恢复」里关掉),备份文件存在你自己的 iCloud 云盘,由 Apple 保管。我们拿不到你的 Apple 账户、付款方式、备份文件或任何账本内容。
- **无账号、无登录、无服务器。** 账本存储在你设备本地(Apple SwiftData),我们没有接收这些数据的服务器。
- **无第三方。** App 不含任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK,仅使用 Apple 系统框架。
- **数据由你掌控。** 你可随时导出 JSON 备份。删除 App 会删除设备上的账本;iCloud 云盘里的备份可以在「文件」App 或 iOS 的 iCloud 设置里删除。

由于我们不收集任何数据,也就不存在数据共享、出售或第三方访问。

如有疑问,可通过 GitHub Issues 联系:https://github.com/coni555/FreeGrid-Freedom/issues

## English

FreeGrid is an expense-tracking app whose ledger stays on your device. Our privacy commitment is simple:

- **No data collection.** FreeGrid does not collect, upload, or share any personal or financial data.
- **Your ledger is never sent to us.** The app uses the network in two places: purchasing or restoring FreeGrid Pro, handled by Apple's App Store; and iCloud Auto Backup (on by default, and you can turn it off in Backup & Restore), which saves backup files to your own iCloud Drive, kept by Apple. We never receive your Apple account, payment details, backup files, or any ledger content.
- **No account, no login, no server.** Your ledger is stored locally on your device (Apple SwiftData), and we have no server that receives this data.
- **No third parties.** No third-party analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs — only Apple system frameworks.
- **You own your data.** Export a JSON backup anytime. Deleting the app deletes the ledger on your device; backups in iCloud Drive can be deleted in the Files app or in iOS iCloud settings.

Because we collect no data, there is no data sharing, selling, or third-party access.
