# FreeGrid 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-06-17

## 中文

FreeGrid 是一款纯本地的记账应用。我们对隐私的承诺很简单:

- **不收集任何数据。** FreeGrid 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人信息或财务数据。
- **零网络。** App 不包含任何网络请求代码,你的数据从不离开你的设备。
- **无账号、无登录、无云。** 所有数据仅存储在你设备本地(Apple SwiftData),只有你能访问。
- **无第三方。** App 不含任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK,仅使用 Apple 系统框架。
- **数据由你掌控。** 你可随时导出 JSON 备份。删除 App 即彻底删除所有数据——这是隐私的代价,也是隐私的保证。

由于我们不收集任何数据,也就不存在数据共享、出售或第三方访问。

如有疑问,可通过 GitHub Issues 联系:https://github.com/coni555/FreeGrid-Freedom/issues

## English

FreeGrid is a fully local expense-tracking app. Our privacy commitment is simple:

- **No data collection.** FreeGrid does not collect, upload, or share any personal or financial data.
- **Zero networking.** The app contains no network code; your data never leaves your device.
- **No account, no login, no cloud.** All data is stored locally on your device (Apple SwiftData) and only you can access it.
- **No third parties.** No third-party analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs — only Apple system frameworks.
- **You own your data.** Export a JSON backup anytime. Deleting the app permanently deletes all data.

Because we collect no data, there is no data sharing, selling, or third-party access.
